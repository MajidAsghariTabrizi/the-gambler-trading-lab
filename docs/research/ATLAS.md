## The story in six acts
1. **Structured rules (2025-12)** — earliest experiments; no surviving edge.
2. **Smart Trader production (2025-11→2026-09)** — 9-expert ensemble + router + calibrated uncertainty gates. The Oct-2026 forensic audit proved: router redundancy (0 action flips V0–V4, effective dim 2.755), gates saturated protectively (100% abstention while C1/C2 ran −24…−28 bps), and one sealed experiment (EXP-004) still untouched.
3. **Cost awakening (Genesis V2/V3)** — paper trades averaged −28.99 bps; V3 audited over 10,478 minutes produced **zero BUYs** (max forecast +4.18 bps vs a +10 bps buffer).
4. **Fusion & challenger reality-checks** — Victory Fusion C1/C2 *lost to a constant*; the P3 purged challenger also lost (24.55 vs 23.55). Promotion stayed gated.
5. **Execution research (P4/P5)** — 3-market frontier; maker fills provably non-identifiable from available data; one hypothesis frozen for prospective evaluation.
6. **The verdict** — no positive-expectancy strategy exists in this program; NO_TRADE is the active, justified policy.
