# The Gambler Trading Lab — Evidence Observatory

**The Trading AI That Knows When NOT to Trade.**

## Read the Complete Research Study

**[From Predictive Accuracy to Executable Alpha — A Longitudinal Case Study of a Cost-Aware Trading Research System](research/from-prediction-to-executable-alpha/)**

One year of trading research. 17 registry entries, 7 trained models, 8 registered experiments — and a registered forward evaluation that established **no profitable edge** for the tested taker policies, with every headline number independently reconciled from raw ledgers.

## Three findings, three populations

| Finding | Value | Population |
|---|---|---|
| Best directional accuracy | 54.60% vs 53.34% control (CI crosses zero) | dev benchmark |
| Registered forward AUC | 0.5683 (n=20,185 matured pairs) | Nov-9 registered window |
| Registered net expectancy | **−42.53 bps/trade, 0/14 positive days** | cov5 taker policy, modeled |
